# Release 0.1.0 — research foundation

## Included

An 80-item audit and 46-chapter crosswalk; 42 source cards with inspection limitations; 31 bounded evidence records; twelve deep-time/prehistory and seven documentary-period dossiers; annual Markdown navigation from 300 BCE through 2026 CE without year zero; documentation, contribution templates, licensing policy and offline build/validation tests.

## Content status

Only 18 annual pages have year-specific seed links. The other 2,308 annual pages are intentionally unresearched. Several populated pages concern research or digitization milestones, not contemporary territorial events. No century, archive or collection is claimed complete. Every seed is pending independent human review.

## Important corrections

The 6000 BCE Edakkal date is no longer treated as established. “About 250 BCE” is no longer presented as an exact start to recorded history. Basement, inherited-mineral, metamorphic, surface and coastline ages are separated. The 2006 geological erratum is a blocking follow-up. Pattanam’s site chronology is not generalized to all the territory, and Pattanam/Muziris remains an attributed identification. Statutory territorial exclusions in 1956 are explicit.

## Engineering limits

No automatic harvesting, live external link checker, OCR engine, general-purpose calendar converter, complete EDTF implementation, deployed website or GIS classifier is included. The scripts validate structure and local references, not historical truth or copyright status. No remote repository has been published.

## Publication gate

Acquire and verify the geographic frame, configure real maintainers and correction reporting, review licensing, and obtain independent review of the anchor claims. Do not present this seed as an exhaustive public history without those checks and a much larger collection inventory.
