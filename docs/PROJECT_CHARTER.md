# Project charter: a history of territory, not of a name

## Purpose

Build a publicly inspectable record of what can be learned about the material territory now within Kerala’s administrative boundary. Begin with geological ancestry and the evidence for past environments; continue through hominin presence, societies, institutions and the contemporary world. Include records produced outside the territory when their contents materially concern it. Do not filter by the word Kerala, a current language, a religious origin story or a dynastic identity.

The finished project should support three different uses: reading a chronological history, checking the evidentiary basis of a particular assertion, and discovering the underlying documents or specimens. No single long narrative can do all three reliably. The repository therefore distinguishes **evidence records**, **sources and their versions**, and **chronological views**.

## What “from the beginning” means

The Earth-age estimate is a global contextual starting point, not a local sample date [S001](../sources/S001.md). The meaningful local chain begins with the oldest interpretable material now present in the study area. A mineral may preserve an earlier history than the rock that incorporates it. A rock may predate the surface on which it is exposed. A terrace can be younger than the material eroded into it. The present coastline and political boundary are later frames, not eternal objects.

Consequently the repository should not search for one mythical birthday of the land. It should reconstruct distinguishable processes. A global-origin prelude may be included, but it must not overwhelm the place-specific evidence. Twelve starting dossiers are provided; their boundaries can change as the corpus improves.

## Three linked historical clocks

There is a chronology of the world being studied, a chronology of the evidence, and a chronology of our knowledge. An engraving can be undated while its published description is precisely dated. An ancient text may describe earlier events and survive only in a later copy. A modern study may revise the date assigned by an older study. The data model must allow all those statements to coexist without collapsing their dates.

This is why year files are generated indexes. They contain exact or explicitly qualified year-level records and link to wider-period evidence. They do not manufacture a year for every statement. An annual file with no entered item says “unresearched”; it does not pronounce the year historically empty.

## Inclusion without geographical distortion

Evidence inside the current territorial frame is core. Outside evidence can be an external reference, an action affecting the area, a source-custody history, a trade connection or regional geological context. An inclusion decision needs a reason, not merely a keyword match.

The 1956 Act illustrates the problem: it includes specified territories and excludes others. A historic administrative unit cannot be imported wholesale as a modern spatial definition [S023](../sources/S023.md). The present Mahe administration is part of Puducherry, despite its physical proximity and historical connections [S024](../sources/S024.md). Rivers and maritime networks will often require a contextual area larger than the land boundary.

For deep time, the modern polygon identifies the material to be investigated. Reconstruction follows those rocks and blocks through movement; it does not pretend that they always occupied their present coordinates.

## What counts as a record

Eligible evidence can include dated specimens and laboratory measurements; excavation reports and field records; inscriptions, coins, seals and manuscripts; maps, deeds, proceedings and gazettes; censuses and statistical series; newspapers and periodicals; photographs, sound and film; oral-history recordings; material and environmental surveys; and modern scholarship that interprets those materials.

These are different kinds of evidence. A gazette tells us that a measure was formally published; implementation needs another record. A traveller’s assertion can be evidence of the assertion without being reliable evidence of its subject. A legend is evidence for its transmission and reception, not automatically for the narrated event. A scientific paper reports an analysis whose methods and later corrections need scrutiny.

## Scope of people and themes

Every era should ask about livelihood, landscape, mobility, power, inequality, gender, technology, belief and knowledge. Histories of Adivasi communities, oppressed castes, women, fishers, agricultural workers, artisans and migrants are not optional closing chapters. Their absence from accessible documentation must be visible as a source problem, not silently treated as historical insignificance.

The same principle includes people not identified with the modern region: temporary residents, displaced people, visiting merchants, labour migrants, rulers elsewhere whose decisions affected the territory, and diasporic relationships where the local connection is documented.

## A realistic meaning of exhaustive

The goal is exhaustive discovery, cataloguing and evidentiary indexing within successively declared digital corpora. It is not an excuse to copy protected material, bypass access restrictions or pretend the universe of online records has a fixed size. A collection can be completely inventoried as of a date even while other relevant collections remain unknown.

Completeness must be multidimensional: catalogue coverage, item access, relevant-page inspection, extraction, translation, rights clearance and independent review. A large number of empty annual files is not evidence of content coverage. A complete crawl is not proof of historical truth.

## What this release actually establishes

It creates the repository structure, source-provenance conventions, annual index, open era dossiers and a limited set of source-linked records. It corrects unsupported precision in the earlier chat and explicitly identifies unresolved primary-source work. It does not produce an exhaustive history of any century or archive.

The authoritative release counts are in [coverage](../coverage/README.md). All human scholarly review is pending. A verified and licensed current boundary dataset is also pending. These are release gates, not hidden assumptions.
