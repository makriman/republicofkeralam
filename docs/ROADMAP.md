# Roadmap and release gates

## Foundation delivered, but not completion

The initial release creates an operational Markdown index, canonical seed records, the prior-chat audit and evidence conventions. The authoritative counts are in [coverage](../coverage/README.md). A generated page is a navigational asset, not evidence that the corresponding year has been researched.

## Gate 1 — define the geographic frame

Acquire and license a current boundary polygon; compare its treatment of enclaves, islands and the coast with authoritative administrative material. Version its source and checksum. Document historical-to-modern place relationships. Leave spatial inclusion provisional until this gate passes.

## Gate 2 — settle the high-risk anchor claims

Retrieve the Trivandrum paper’s erratum and sample context; evaluate the Hadean/ancient-crust lead without confusing model ages and rock ages. Retrieve primary north-Kerala Palaeolithic and Thenmala/Ankode reports, including dating records. Build Edakkal panel/inscription records rather than accepting a tourism date.

Compare the early documentary witness in critical editions. Keep Ashoka’s third-century horizon, proposed exact dates and geographic identification distinct. Review earlier literary candidates without backdating a text to the period of its story.

## Gate 3 — finish one bounded pilot corpus

Choose a small, enumerated collection for which item access and relevant review skills are available. A volume of inscriptions or a narrowly defined archaeological report series is preferable to an undefined “all Kerala history” search. Publish its item manifest, pages reviewed, missing pieces, rights decisions and extracted claim register. A completed pilot is a method demonstration, not the completion of an era.

## Gate 4 — build the multilingual primary-source spine

Create separate workstreams for geological measurements, archaeological reports, epigraphy, literary witnesses, early travel/commercial documents, medieval local institutions, colonial administrative records and modern public series. For each, recruit the relevant language and disciplinary review rather than one generalist queue. Preserve source dependency and disagreement.

## Gate 5 — populate annual history without fabricated precision

Enter supported year-level records first. Keep century ranges and undated evidence in dossiers. Add research and digitization milestones under separate headings. Each annual page needs a coverage statement that can differ by theme and collection. Do not infer that a page is complete because it is long.

## Gate 6 — publish a reviewable public release

Configure a remote repository, real maintainer details, contribution review, correction reporting and backup/release practices. No remote repository has been created by this package. Human reviewers should verify the licence policy and the provenance model before large-scale ingestion.

The starter does not promise automated external harvesting, calendar conversion, OCR pipelines or a public website. Those are separately scoped engineering tasks. First prove a small source-to-record-to-year workflow with reproducible review.

## Research strands that must remain visible

Geology and past life; climate and coastal change; Adivasi histories and land; farming, fishing, forests and crafts; migration and multilingual contact; religion and institutions; caste, slavery, labour and gender; political authority and resistance; public health and education; technology, scholarship and artistic production; ecology, urbanization and contemporary vulnerabilities.

These strands are not period-specific appendices. Maintain a coverage matrix across time, locality, source genre, language and community. Missing evidence and digital exclusion should appear explicitly.

## Completion criteria for an individual source item

A stable identifier and provenance exist; relevant content and its version are known; geographical relation is justified; date types are preserved; claims have page-level support; rights and ethical treatment are stated; contradictory evidence has been sought at a documented scope; and appropriate human review is recorded. Failure of a gate does not delete the item: it stays as a clearly labelled lead.
